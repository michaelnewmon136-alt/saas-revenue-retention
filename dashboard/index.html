What the simulation shows (from the SQL investigation)

  • Usage falls step by step before churn: about 59% of plan norm one month before cancelling, versus about 102% otherwise.
  • About half of accounts with an escalated ticket cancelled the next month (51.7%) versus 3.0% baseline, but 60% of churners had no escalation.
  • Enterprise accounts for the largest share of contraction MRR.
These patterns were planted in the generator. They show the pipeline detects them. They are correlations, not proof that raising usage or fixing tickets prevents churn. The risk flag is a proposed rule, not a validated prediction.
